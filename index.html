The Model

Humans are biological organisms shaped by 3.5 billion years of evolution and 300,000 years of mostly hunter-gatherer life, with selection still at work, now living in environments that have changed faster than evolution can keep up with. We run on a small set of universal drives (survival, reproduction, status, belonging, meaning, understanding, autonomy, comfort) inside a brain where fast emotional processing often runs ahead of slow, deliberate reasoning. We cooperate at scale through shared stories and institutions: money, gods, nations, brands, laws. The mismatch between our nature and our environment produces much modern dysfunction, and much of it can be addressed by changing inputs rather than fighting biology.

The five core claims

The whole document in five sentences.

If the paragraph above is too long, here are the five claims you can carry in your head. Each one is unpacked across an entire chapter in the source.

01

You are a Pleistocene primate.

Your body and mind were shaped in a different world. Many of your reflexes were tuned for scarcity and small groups, though some have changed since. Your fitness genes, your status anxiety, your tribal loyalty, your cravings. Ancestral defaults running on 21st century inputs.

02

Status matters more than most adults will admit.

The desire for status is a fundamental motive, seen across cultures, ages and personalities. It shapes behavior dressed up as principle, and much of what looks like ideology or preference is partly rank pursuit. Naming it stops it from running you.

03

Cooperation at scale runs on shared fictions and learned norms.

Money, brands, nations, laws. They are not lies. They are the technology that lets strangers cooperate. They work as long as enough people believe them. Civilization is fragile because its operating system is belief.

04

Much dysfunction is mismatch.

The body is doing what it was built to do, and some inputs are wrong. Many modern problems (obesity, loneliness, distraction, stress) are ancestral systems responding to inputs they were not built for. Change the inputs and many of them improve, as with food and sitting. For others the cause is less clear.

05

The good life is not a default.

It has to be constructed deliberately, against ancient drives that no longer fit and modern environments that exploit them. The biology will not deliver flourishing by accident. The environment will not deliver it by default. Conscious participation is required.

"

Every drive, instinct, emotion, and preference a human has was selected for because it improved reproductive success in past environments, not because it makes the human happy or wise in the present.

From Chapter 02. The Hardware
The hardware

A brain with fast and slow systems.

Evolution is conservative. It builds on what is already there rather than starting over. The old picture of three stacked brain layers does not hold up. What holds up is a working split: fast, automatic processing that responds first, and slow, deliberate reasoning that can overrule it. When "what I should do" and "what I want to do" fight, you are usually feeling that split.

Basic body control

Brainstem

Heart rate. Breathing. Body temperature. Sleep cycles. Reflexes. Runs without you. You cannot override it consciously beyond very narrow windows.

Emotion and reward

Deep structures

Amygdala, hippocampus, hypothalamus, basal ganglia. Central to emotion, reward and memory: fear, anger, attraction, disgust, joy. Fast and automatic. Many "gut reactions" start here, below awareness.

Deliberate reasoning

Cortex

Reason, language, planning, social modeling, self-reflection. Especially the prefrontal cortex, behind your forehead. Among the last regions to mature, with development continuing into the mid-20s. Less powerful in conflict than people assume.

Fast system, slow system

Emotion reaches into reasoning. The amygdala can steer attention and working memory, so what you feel changes what you notice and weigh. Under stress, deliberate control weakens and fast emotional responses take over. Do not build on willpower alone: the idea that it runs out like fuel has not held up in replications. Set up your environment and habits so your fast defaults already match what you want.

The story

300,000 years of Homo sapiens, in phase transitions.

Each phase transition changed the conditions of human life enough that everything afterward was different. Our basic biology changed little, though selection kept working. The environment changed fast. Much of what troubles us now is downstream of the gap that opened between them.

7 million years ago

The split from chimps

The human lineage diverges from the chimpanzee lineage. There is no single "first human." A chain of hominid species follows: Australopithecus, Homo habilis, Homo erectus. Brains grow. Tools appear. Fire is controlled by roughly 1 million years ago.

300,000 years ago

Homo sapiens appears

We emerge in Africa. We are one of several human species on the planet, including Neanderthals and Denisovans. For 230,000 years we show no obvious cognitive supremacy.

70,000 to 30,000 years ago

The Cognitive Revolution

Art, ornament, finer tools and long-distance trade goods appear in Africa from at least 80,000 years ago and build up gradually, not all at once. Harari calls this the Cognitive Revolution, and many archaeologists see no single leap. Language gains the ability to reference abstractions and fictions. We gain the technology of shared imaginary realities, which lets us cooperate in groups far larger than anyone can know personally. The other human species do not have this capability. Within tens of thousands of years, they are gone.

Until about 12,000 years ago

Hunter-gatherer life

Small bands averaging about 28 adults (30 to 50 people with children), linked to wider networks of related bands. Mostly egalitarian within bands, though not every society fit that pattern. Status earned through skill. Animist worldview, ritual, oral storytelling. This is the world our psychology was largely tuned for. We are still those people, just in a different building.

12,000 years ago

The Agricultural Revolution

Cultivation begins independently in the Fertile Crescent, China, the Indus Valley, Mesoamerica, the Andes, and elsewhere. Surplus makes hierarchy, property, taxes, kings, armies, slavery and writing possible. Population explodes. Skeletons show more dental disease and infection than those of earlier foragers. The first big stretching of the gap between our nature and our environment.

5,000 years ago

First civilizations

Sumer. Egypt. The Indus Valley. Shang China. Cities, temples, divine kings, codes of law, long-distance trade, organized warfare, slavery. Patterns that converge across cultures with no contact, because they are the solutions humans reach for under those conditions.

800 to 200 BCE

The Axial Age

Buddha. Confucius. Laozi. The Hebrew prophets. Zoroaster. Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, the Stoics. Independently, in widely separated civilizations, humans begin to ask in writing: how should one live? The traditions of this period still structure the moral and philosophical life of most humans alive today.

500 BCE to 500 CE

Classical empires

Persia, Maurya, Han China, Rome. The first polities governing tens of millions through standing armies, bureaucracy, codified law, paid roads. Universal religions emerge (Christianity, Buddhism spreading east) as the first scalable software for binding strangers into a moral community.

500 to 1500 CE

The medieval world

Islam emerges and spans Spain to Central Asia within a century. The Islamic Golden Age preserves Greek learning and advances mathematics and medicine. Song China invents paper money, printing, gunpowder. The Mongol Empire briefly connects Asia and Europe. The Black Death kills a third to over half of Europe. Universities, parliaments, charters of rights begin to appear.

1500 to 1800

Scientific Revolution + Enlightenment

Copernicus. Galileo. Newton. The discovery that observation and math can overrule authority on factual claims. The Enlightenment extends the method to politics, law, religion, economics. Locke, Hume, Smith, Kant. Natural rights, government by consent, the idea of progress. The modern world is born in its values before it is born in its conditions.

1750 to 1900

Industrial Revolution

Steam, coal, iron, electricity. Energy densities no prior society had access to. Productivity rises by orders of magnitude. Cities explode. Family structure changes. Slavery is abolished across the Atlantic world. Europe colonizes most of the planet by 1900. The material world reshapes faster than at any prior moment.

The 20th century

Ideologies, decolonization, IT, mass life expectancy

Communism, fascism, liberal democracy compete. Liberal democracy plus markets emerges dominant by 1991. Empires dissolve. Antibiotics, vaccines, sanitation add more than 30 years to American life expectancy. Two existential technologies appear: nuclear weapons, then biotechnology. The gap between human nature and human environment widens beyond anything prior.

Now

A phase transition in progress

Globalization peaks and partially reverses. Smartphones connect most of humanity. Heavy social media use goes with lower teen wellbeing, though the link is small and its cause is debated. AI emerges as a general-purpose technology, reshaping software and knowledge work in real time. Climate change is in the data, not the forecast. Geopolitical tensions higher than any point since the Cold War. The drives have not changed.

We are still those people. Just in a different building.

The drives

Eight motivations that shape adult behavior.

Cultural surface varies dramatically across centuries and continents. The underlying drives are remarkably stable. All eight are active simultaneously in any adult life, weighing against each other in moment-to-moment decisions. Predicting human behavior means asking which drive is dominant right now and what the others are doing in the background.

Drive 01

Survival

Air, water, food, warmth, sleep, safety from immediate threat. When unmet, almost nothing else matters. In modern industrialized societies these are met by default for most people. The system still scans for threats it cannot find and manufactures substitutes: a snippy email, a news headline, a social slight.

Drive 02

Reproduction and sex

The most obvious evolutionary drive and the least openly discussed. Hardwired below conscious awareness. An enormous amount of human display, accomplishment, competition, and creative production is mating-related, even when the performer would not describe it that way.

A core drive

Drive 03. Status

Your rank within the group you care about. A fundamental driver of adult behavior, and the least admitted one. In many social mammals rank shapes health and access to mates and food, though whether the top or the bottom is more stressed depends on how the group is organized. Losing standing is a strong stressor, and feeling defeated tracks depression closely. A lot of what people do, including what they call principle, is partly status competition in disguise.

Drive 04

Belonging and tribe

Humans evolved as group animals. A human alone is a human at risk: people without close, stable relationships show worse health and well-being. Social rejection hurts, activating some of the same brain regions as physical pain. Across 148 studies, people with stronger social ties were 50 percent more likely to survive the follow-up period. The drive forms tribes around any reliable identity marker: language, dress, religion, sports team, political party, profession.

Drive 05

Meaning and significance

Once basic needs are met, meaning becomes the bottleneck. Three sources are well documented: belonging to something larger than oneself, contributing through effort, and having a coherent narrative about your life. When all three are present, meaning is high. When any is missing, meaning falters.

Drive 06

Understanding and mastery

Curiosity in its surface form, mastery in its longer form. Tied to dopamine, which responds most to results better than expected. Curiosity is fragile, extinguished by tedium, humiliation, or premature answers. Mastery requires deliberate practice at the edge of current ability. The foundation of every human accomplishment in science, art, craft, and sport.

Drive 07

Autonomy and control

The desire to be the cause of your own actions, not the object of someone else's. Cross-culturally stable. How work is designed, including how much control people have over how, when, and where it happens, explains about a third of job satisfaction across 259 studies. Pay correlates with it at only .15. The autonomy drive is in tension with belonging; healthy adult life is a negotiation between the two.

Drive 08

Comfort and pleasure

The simplest drive, and the one our modern environment hijacks most successfully. The dopamine reward system evolved to teach the brain "this is worth pursuing." In abundance, products are built to exploit that system: engineered foods, algorithmic feeds, gambling, pornography. Whether heavy use counts as addiction is disputed. The drive is not pathological. The environment changed.

Status is one of the strongest and least admitted drivers of adult human behavior. Much of what people do, including a lot of what they think is principled, is partly status competition in disguise.

The universals

Traits found in every human society ever studied.

In 1991, anthropologist Donald Brown published a list of human universals. Hundreds of items shared across every culture carefully observed. If a feature shows up in pre-agricultural hunter-gatherers, ancient Sumerian cities, medieval China, and modern Manhattan, it is part of human nature, not a cultural accident.

Language

Grammar with nouns and verbs. Tense or its equivalents. Logical operators (and, not, true, false). Translation always possible.

Music + dance

Group song. Rhythm. Dance. Lullabies. Music tied to ritual.

Family + kinship

Marriage in some form. Incest taboos. Pair bonding. Mother, father, child roles. Extended kin networks.

Sex + reproduction

Sexual jealousy. Modesty about some behaviors. Concepts of beauty. Pubertal customs.

Politics + law

Leadership. Conflict resolution. Property. Inheritance. Rules. Punishment.

Religion + ritual

Belief in supernatural agents. Rituals around birth, death, transitions. Magical thinking. Concepts of soul or spirit.

Morality

Fairness sense. Reciprocity. Empathy. In-group vs out-group moral standards.

Aesthetics

Body decoration. Visual art. Concepts of beauty. Color terms in almost every language.

Tools

Tool use. Tool-making. Containers. Cooking. Fire. Weapons. Clothing.

Economic life

Exchange. Specialization by sex and age. Reciprocal gift-giving. Trade. Property.

Knowledge

Folk biology. Folk physics. Folk psychology. Beliefs about death and disease.

Social structure

In-group vs out-group. Status hierarchies. Sanctions. Distinct gender roles.

What this means

Religion, hierarchy, gender roles, property, art, storytelling, and conflict are universal in form, varied in detail. No society without supernatural beliefs has ever been documented. No society without status differences either. The strict 20th-century claims that gender is fully constructed or that property is artificial are not supported by the cross-cultural record. The opposite biological-determinist claims also fail. Both biology and culture are real, and both do meaningful work.

Individual variation

Within the universals, the Big Five.

Five dimensions of personality that replicate across most large-scale societies studied (not among the Tsimane, a Bolivian forager-horticulturalist group). Each is roughly 40 to 60 percent heritable. Fairly stable in adulthood, and more so with age, but never frozen. The trait is your starting place, not your identity. Working with it beats fighting it.

The mismatch

Evolved nature against modern environment.

Mismatch theory is one of the most useful lenses for modern psychological dysfunction. Many chronic problems are not pathologies of the system. They are the predictable response of an ancestral system to inputs it was not built for. Our basic biology changed little, though selection kept working. The environment changed fast. Many effective interventions change the environment, not the person.

The pattern

Each row is the same story. An ancient system optimized for one environment is now receiving inputs from a different one. The dysfunction is not random. It is what a well-functioning ancient program produces on inputs it was not built for. Often the biology is fine. The inputs are wrong.

Common mistakes

Five errors that will trip you up.

When you start exploring human nature, you will encounter these failure patterns. Each one is half-right and dangerous when taken as a full explanation. Naming them in advance prevents most of the damage.

Error 01

The blank slate

The claim that humans are born with no predispositions and that all behavior comes from culture. Largely false. We are born with rich evolved predispositions: face preference, language acquisition order, quick detection of snakes, moral intuitions. Culture builds on this scaffolding, sometimes dramatically. It does not write on an empty page.

Error 02

Biological determinism

The opposite mistake. The claim that biology fully determines behavior and culture is decoration. Also false. Biology sets ranges and defaults. Within those ranges, culture has enormous power. Both are real, and you need both to explain anything.

Error 03

The noble savage

The idea that pre-civilized humans lived in peace and that civilization corrupted us. Hunter-gatherer life had real strengths: egalitarianism, healthy nutrition, strong bonds. It also had real violence, high child mortality, no medicine. Scholars disagree on how much of it was organized warfare and how much personal killing. It was not Eden.

Error 04

Modern people are different

The mirror image. The claim that we are categorically different from people 2,000 or 20,000 years ago because of iPhones or science. Wrong. Selection has kept working, but our basic drives, emotions and social needs are recognizably those of a Bronze Age farmer. Read Homer. You will recognize yourself, though reasoning styles and moral priorities differ across populations.

Error 05

One driver explains everything

The temptation to reduce behavior to one master variable. Sex, money, status, fear of death, power, class struggle, rational choice. Every major theorist who tried this is partly right and largely wrong. Human behavior is overdetermined. Multiple drives are always active. A theory that reduces everything to one will miss most of the picture.

Bonus

Romanticizing the future

Progress is real but not guaranteed. Technology does not automatically improve human wellbeing. Recent technologies (engineered food, social media, dating apps) have produced significant new harms alongside their benefits. Assume the next technology will too, even if you like it.

Stability and change

What history changes, what it doesn't.

Most stable features of your current life depend on continued operation of cooperation infrastructure. Most stable features of your inner life depend on continued operation of your biology. Plan for the second to be more reliable than the first.

Has changed dramatically

Conditions

Material conditions (food, shelter, medicine, transport). Communication speed. Average lifespan and infant mortality. Slavery, formal hierarchy, gender expectations. Political institutions (democracy, citizenship, rights). Religious landscape. Long-run violence rates (Pinker documents a fall, though some statisticians dispute it).

All of these have shifted enormously over centuries. Most of what feels permanent about your current society is recent and contingent.

Has stayed essentially the same

Nature

The drives: status, sex, belonging, meaning, comfort. The basic emotional palette. Cognitive architecture (fast, automatic judgment running ahead of slow, deliberate reasoning, much of it unconscious). The need for tribe and ritual. Patterns of cooperation and defection. Civilizations rising and falling, which some historians read as recurring cycles (a disputed view). The reality of compounding asymmetries.

These have been stable for thousands of years and will be stable for the foreseeable future.

"

Civilization is a technology for cooperating at scale despite our nature. It is real, it is fragile, and it is not guaranteed to continue.

The minimum viable protocol

Ten infrastructure habits.

Most adult life optimization is not adding more. It is removing the wrong inputs and rebuilding the right ones. These ten habits cost little, and trials support exercise, sleep and lifestyle change as ways to improve mood and attention.

01

Sleep

Consistent schedule. Dark bedroom. No screens an hour before bed. Morning sunlight. Aim for 7 to 9 hours. One of the highest-leverage interventions in modern health: better sleep reliably lifts mood.

02

Movement

Walk daily, ideally outdoor. Resistance training at least twice a week. Break up long stretches of sitting with a few minutes of movement. The body interprets prolonged inactivity as a survival signal that conditions are bad.

03

Food

Whole foods most of the time. Restrict ultra-processed food at the source by not buying it. Adequate protein. Fiber. The fix is not "eat less." The fix is changing the food environment.

04

Light

Bright light in the morning, ideally outdoor within an hour of waking. Dim light in the evening. Outdoor time every day. The circadian system is calibrated against the sun.

05

Connection

Two to four close relationships maintained actively. A weekly in-person ritual with someone you care about. Solitary humans deteriorate. This is not optional. This is the substrate.

06

Information diet

Sharp limits on feed-based content. Replace with chosen sources: books, specific creators, long-form. Phone outside the bedroom. Build the capacity for boredom back. It is the substrate of creativity.

07

Status

Choose your hierarchies deliberately. Curate your feed to remove unproductive comparisons. Inventory the status games you are playing. Decide which to invest in, which to exit.

08

Meaning

A practice or project you find inherently worthwhile, protected on the calendar. Most adults underweight meaning in favor of urgent demands. The cost is delayed and severe.

09

Stress recovery

Real rest, not media consumption. Walks. Time in nature. Sleep. Sauna. Breath work. Whatever closes the stress cycle for you. Modern life keeps reopening it. You have to close it deliberately.

10

Mating

Treat your relationship or pursuit of one as a serious life project, not a hobby. Avoid substitutes that drain the drive without satisfying it. The infrastructure for stable pair bonds has weakened. Conscious construction is required.

What these habits are

A foundation, not a cure. Serious mental illness often has biological components that require treatment. This is the substrate on which good mental health rests, and modern life has quietly subtracted much of it. Reinstating it is among the highest-leverage personal interventions available.

"

Cultivate what your biology wants. Override what your environment exploits.

What the model does not solve

Where this model is uncertain.

A good model names its own limits. Some honest uncertainties to carry alongside what the rest of the document claims confidently.

The cognitive revolution mechanism

Was it genetic, cultural, or both? The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive. FOXP2, once the lead suspect, no longer fits, and many archaeologists see a gradual build-up rather than one leap. The candidates are a genetic change, a cultural threshold, or a mix of the two.

Consciousness itself

None of the models in the source touch the hard problem. Why subjective experience exists at all remains open, and most of what we say about psychology is silent on it.

Genes vs environment, in any specific case

Population-level heritability estimates do not translate cleanly to individual cases. Knowing that a trait is 50 percent heritable in a population tells you almost nothing about what is possible for a specific person.

Humans deeply embedded in artificial environments

People who have grown up entirely online may have differently calibrated drives. We do not yet know what that looks like at population scale. Teen wellbeing data is the early signal, and researchers disagree about how much screens explain.

AI's effect on cooperation infrastructure

Money, contracts, brands, identity. All may be transformed by AI in the next 10 to 20 years. The drives will not change. The institutions that channel them will.

Whether the rise-and-fall cycle has the same shape now

Some historians see cycles. The current moment is unlike any prior one in some specific ways: global connectivity, technological acceleration. The cycle may compress, stretch, or change form.

The model is robust enough to bet on. It is not robust enough to claim certainty. Use it accordingly.

Closing

You are a biological organism with finite time and a set of drives that, properly fed, can produce flourishing.

The drives, properly understood, can also produce a life that looked busy and felt empty. You get about 4,000 weeks if you live to 80 (Oliver Burkeman), and you have spent a substantial fraction of them already. The remaining ones are finite and not refundable.

This is not a depressing observation. It is a clarifying one. The drives that are loudest in any moment are not always the drives that matter on a 30-year time horizon. The status game that consumes you now will be irrelevant in 10 years. The relationships you are neglecting now will be the ones you wish you had cultivated. The contribution you keep postponing is the one you will regret.

Use the model. Add to it. Update it when reality demands.

Če preberete eno samo stvar.

Strnjen odstavek, ki vsebuje celoten model. Vse drugo na tej strani so dokazi, struktura in uporaba tega, kar sledi.

Model

Ljudje smo biološki organizmi, ki nas je oblikovalo 3,5 milijarde let evolucije in 300.000 let pretežno lovsko-nabiralniškega življenja. Naravni izbor deluje še naprej, mi pa danes živimo v okoljih, ki so se spremenila hitreje, kot jim evolucija zmore slediti. Poganja nas peščica univerzalnih gonov (preživetje, razmnoževanje, status, pripadnost, smisel, razumevanje, avtonomija, udobje), v možganih pa hitri čustveni odzivi pogosto prehitijo počasno, preudarno razmišljanje. V velikem obsegu sodelujemo prek skupnih zgodb in ustanov: denar, bogovi, narodi, znamke, zakoni. Neujemanje med našo naravo in okoljem poraja velik del sodobnih motenj in precej jih lahko omilimo tako, da spremenimo vhodne podatke, namesto da bi se borili proti biologiji.

Pet temeljnih trditev

Celoten dokument v petih stavkih.

Če je zgornji odstavek predolg, je tu pet trditev, ki jih lahko nosite v glavi. Vsaka od njih je v izvorniku razdelana v celotnem poglavju.

01

Ste pleistocenski primat.

Vaše telo in vaš um sta se oblikovala v drugačnem svetu. Mnogi vaši refleksi so bili naravnani na pomanjkanje in majhne skupine, čeprav so se nekateri od takrat spremenili. Vaši geni za telesno pripravljenost, tesnoba glede statusa, plemenska zvestoba in poželenje so pradavne privzete nastavitve, ki zdaj obdelujejo vhodne podatke 21. stoletja.

02

Status je pomembnejši, kot bi večina odraslih priznala.

Želja po statusu je temeljni motiv, prisoten ne glede na kulturo, starost in osebnost. Oblikuje tudi vedenje, ki se izdaja za načelnost, in marsikaj, kar je videti kot ideologija ali osebni okus, je deloma preoblečeno prizadevanje za status. Ko ta motiv poimenujete, vas ne vodi več.

03

Sodelovanje v velikem obsegu sloni na skupnih fikcijah in naučenih normah.

Denar, znamke, narodi, zakoni. To niso laži. So tehnologija, ki neznancem omogoča sodelovanje. Delujejo, dokler vanje verjame dovolj ljudi. Civilizacija je krhka, ker je njen operacijski sistem verovanje.

04

Velik del motenj izvira iz neujemanja.

Telo počne to, za kar se je razvilo, nekateri vhodni podatki pa so napačni. Za mnogimi sodobnimi težavami (debelost, osamljenost, raztresenost, stres) so pradavni sistemi, ki se odzivajo na vhodne podatke, za katere niso bili zgrajeni. Ko spremenite vhodne podatke, se mnoge od njih omilijo, kar dobro kažeta prehrana in sedenje. Pri drugih je vzrok manj jasen.

05

Dobro življenje ni privzeta nastavitev.

Zgraditi ga je treba premišljeno, proti starodavnim gonom, ki se ne prilegajo več, in proti sodobnim okoljem, ki jih izkoriščajo. Biologija razcveta ne bo prinesla po naključju. Okolje ga ne bo prineslo samo od sebe. Potrebno je zavestno sodelovanje.

Iz 2. poglavja. Strojna oprema
Strojna oprema

Možgani s hitrim in počasnim sistemom.

Evolucija je konservativna. Gradi na tem, kar že obstaja, namesto da bi začenjala znova. Stara predstava o treh plasteh možganov, naloženih druga na drugo, ne zdrži. Uporabna pa ostaja delitev na dvoje: hitra, samodejna obdelava, ki se odzove prva, in počasno, preudarno razmišljanje, ki jo lahko preglasi. Ko se v vas spopadeta »moral bi« in »rad bi«, običajno čutite prav to delitev.

Osnovne telesne funkcije

Možgansko deblo

Srčni utrip. Dihanje. Telesna temperatura. Cikli spanja. Refleksi. Vse to deluje samo od sebe, zavestno pa lahko nanj vplivate le v zelo ozkih mejah.

Čustva in nagrajevanje

Globoke možganske strukture

Amigdala, hipokampus, hipotalamus, bazalni gangliji. Ključni so za čustva, nagrajevanje in spomin: strah, jeza, privlačnost, gnus, veselje. Delujejo hitro in samodejno. Marsikateri »nagonski odziv« se sproži tu, pod ravnijo zavedanja.

Preudarno razmišljanje

Skorja

Razum, jezik, načrtovanje, modeliranje družbenih odnosov, samorefleksija. Zlasti prefrontalna skorja tik za čelom. Dozori med zadnjimi, saj se razvija vse do srede dvajsetih let. V spopadu s čustvi je šibkejša, kot si mislimo.

Hitri sistem, počasni sistem

Čustva posegajo v razmišljanje. Amigdala lahko usmerja pozornost in delovni spomin, zato to, kar čutite, vpliva na to, kaj opazite in čemu pripišete težo. Pod stresom zavestni nadzor oslabi in vajeti prevzamejo hitri čustveni odzivi. Ne zanašajte se le na moč volje: zamisel, da se izčrpa kot gorivo, se v ponovljenih raziskavah ni potrdila. Okolje in navade si uredite tako, da se bodo vaši hitri, samodejni odzivi že sami ujemali s tem, kar si želite.

Zgodba

300.000 let vrste Homo sapiens, v faznih prehodih.

Vsak fazni prehod je tako močno spremenil pogoje človeškega življenja, da je bilo potem vse drugače. Naša osnovna biologija se je le malo spremenila, čeprav je naravni izbor deloval še naprej. Okolje pa se je spreminjalo hitro. Velik del tega, kar nas danes pesti, izvira iz vrzeli, ki se je odprla med njima.

Pred 7 milijoni let

Ločitev od šimpanzov

Človeška veja se razide s šimpanzovsko. Nobenega enega samega »prvega človeka« ni. Sledi veriga hominidnih vrst: Australopithecus, Homo habilis, Homo erectus. Možgani rastejo. Pojavijo se orodja. Ogenj ukrotijo približno pred milijonom let.

Pred 300.000 leti

Pojavi se Homo sapiens

Vzniknemo v Afriki. Smo ena od več človeških vrst na planetu, med njimi so neandertalci in denisovci. 230.000 let ne kažemo nobene očitne kognitivne premoči.

Pred 70.000 do 30.000 leti

Kognitivna revolucija

Umetnost, okrasje, izpopolnjena orodja in blago za trgovino na dolge razdalje se v Afriki pojavijo vsaj pred 80.000 leti, nato pa se postopoma kopičijo. Harari temu pravi kognitivna revolucija, mnogi arheologi pa enkratnega preskoka ne vidijo. Jezik postane zmožen izražati abstraktne pojme in izmišljene stvari. Pridobimo tehnologijo skupnih namišljenih resničnosti, ki nam omogoča sodelovati v skupinah, precej večjih od kroga ljudi, ki jih posameznik lahko pozna osebno. Druge človeške vrste te zmožnosti nimajo in v nekaj deset tisoč letih izginejo.

Do pred približno 12.000 leti

Lovsko-nabiralniško življenje

Majhne skupine s približno 28 odraslimi v povprečju (skupaj z otroki 30 do 50 ljudi), povezane v širše mreže sorodnih skupin. Znotraj skupin so bili ljudje večinoma enakopravni, čeprav temu vzorcu niso sledile vse družbe. Status so si prislužili s spretnostjo. Animistični svetovni nazor, obredje, ustno pripovedništvo. To je svet, na katerega je v veliki meri uglašena naša psihologija. Še vedno smo isti ljudje, le v drugačni stavbi.

Pred 12.000 leti

Agrarna revolucija

Poljedelstvo se neodvisno razvije v Rodovitnem polmesecu, na Kitajskem, v dolini Inda, v Mezoameriki, v Andih in drugod. Presežki hrane omogočijo hierarhijo, lastnino, davke, kralje, vojske, suženjstvo in pisavo. Število prebivalcev strmo naraste. Na okostjih je več sledi zobnih bolezni in okužb kot pri zgodnejših lovcih in nabiralcih. Vrzel med našo naravo in okoljem se prvič močno razširi.

Pred 5.000 leti

Prve civilizacije

Sumer. Egipt. Dolina Inda. Šangovska Kitajska. Mesta, templji, božanski kralji, zakoniki, trgovina na dolge razdalje, organizirano vojskovanje, suženjstvo. Vzorci, ki se stekajo v kulturah brez medsebojnega stika, ker so to rešitve, po katerih ljudje posežejo pod takimi pogoji.

800 do 200 pr. n. št.

Osna doba

Buda. Konfucij. Lao Ce. Hebrejski preroki. Zaratustra. Sokrat, Platon, Aristotel, stoiki. Neodvisno, v med seboj daleč ločenih civilizacijah, ljudje začnejo pisno spraševati: kako naj človek živi? Izročila tega obdobja še vedno oblikujejo moralno in filozofsko življenje večine danes živečih ljudi.

500 pr. n. št. do 500 n. št.

Klasična cesarstva

Perzija, Maurijci, dinastija Han na Kitajskem, Rim. Prve ureditve, ki so s stalnimi vojskami, birokracijo, kodificiranim pravom in tlakovanimi cestami vladale desetinam milijonov. Vzniknejo univerzalne religije (krščanstvo, budizem, ki se širi proti vzhodu) kot prva programska oprema, sposobna povezati neznance v moralno skupnost v velikem obsegu.

500 do 1500 n. št.

Srednjeveški svet

Vznikne islam in se v stoletju razpne od Španije do Srednje Azije. Islamska zlata doba ohrani grško učenost ter napreduje v matematiki in medicini. Songovska Kitajska iznajde papirnati denar, tisk, smodnik. Mongolski imperij za kratek čas poveže Azijo in Evropo. Črna smrt pobije od tretjine do več kot polovice prebivalcev Evrope. Začnejo se pojavljati univerze, parlamenti, listine pravic.

1500 do 1800

Znanstvena revolucija in razsvetljenstvo

Kopernik. Galilei. Newton. Odkritje, da lahko opazovanje in matematika v dejstvenih vprašanjih preglasita avtoriteto. Razsvetljenstvo razširi metodo na politiko, pravo, religijo, ekonomijo. Locke, Hume, Smith, Kant. Naravne pravice, oblast s privoljenjem, ideja napredka. Sodobni svet se rodi v svojih vrednotah, preden se rodi v svojih razmerah.

1750 do 1900

Industrijska revolucija

Para, premog, železo, elektrika. Gostote energije, do kakršnih ni imela dostopa nobena prejšnja družba. Produktivnost naraste za velikostne rede. Mesta eksplodirajo. Družinska struktura se spremeni. Suženjstvo odpravijo po vsem atlantskem svetu. Do leta 1900 Evropa kolonizira večino planeta. Materialni svet se preoblikuje hitreje kot kadar koli prej.

20. stoletje

Ideologije, dekolonizacija, informatika, množično podaljšanje življenjske dobe

Tekmujejo komunizem, fašizem, liberalna demokracija. Do leta 1991 se kot prevladujoča izlušči liberalna demokracija skupaj s trgi. Imperiji razpadejo. Antibiotiki, cepiva in higiena podaljšajo pričakovano življenjsko dobo v ZDA za več kot 30 let. Pojavita se dve eksistencialni tehnologiji: jedrsko orožje, nato biotehnologija. Vrzel med človeško naravo in človeškim okoljem se razširi bolj kot kdaj prej.

Zdaj

Fazni prehod v teku

Globalizacija doseže vrh in se deloma obrne nazaj. Pametni telefoni povežejo večino človeštva. Pogosta raba družbenih omrežij je povezana s slabšim počutjem mladostnikov, vendar je zveza šibka, vzročnost pa ostaja sporna. Umetna inteligenca vznikne kot tehnologija za splošno rabo in v realnem času preoblikuje programsko opremo in umsko delo. Podnebne spremembe so v podatkih, ne v napovedih. Geopolitične napetosti so višje kot v katerem koli trenutku po hladni vojni. Goni se niso spremenili.

Iz 3. poglavja. Zgodba
Goni

Osem motivov, ki oblikujejo vedenje odraslih.

Kulturna površina se dramatično razlikuje od stoletja do stoletja in od celine do celine. Goni v ozadju so izjemno stabilni. V vsakem odraslem življenju je hkrati dejavnih vseh osem in v vsakem trenutnem odločanju tehtajo drug proti drugemu. Napovedati človeško vedenje pomeni vprašati se, kateri gon je pravkar prevladujoč in kaj počnejo ostali v ozadju.

Gon 01

Preživetje

Zrak, voda, hrana, toplota, spanje, varnost pred neposredno grožnjo. Kadar niso zadovoljeni, skoraj nič drugega ni pomembno. V sodobnih industrializiranih družbah so za večino ljudi zadovoljeni samodejno. Sistem še naprej oprezuje za grožnjami, ki jih ne najde, in si izmišlja nadomestke: osoren e-poštni odgovor, naslov v novicah, družbeno zbadanje.

Gon 02

Razmnoževanje in spolnost

Najbolj očiten evolucijski gon in tisti, o katerem se najmanj odkrito govori. Trdo ožičen pod ravnijo zavesti. Ogromen del človeškega razkazovanja, dosežkov, tekmovanja in ustvarjalne produkcije je povezan s parjenjem, tudi kadar izvajalec tega ne bi tako opisal.

Temeljni gon

Gon 03. Status

Vaš položaj v skupini, za katero vam je mar. Je temeljno gonilo vedenja odraslih in hkrati tisto, ki si ga najmanj priznamo. Pri mnogih družbenih sesalcih položaj vpliva na zdravje ter na dostop do partnerjev in hrane, vendar je od ureditve skupine odvisno, ali so bolj pod stresom tisti na vrhu ali tisti na dnu. Izguba ugleda je močan vir stresa, občutek poraza pa je tesno povezan z depresijo. Velik del tega, kar ljudje počnejo, je deloma preoblečeno tekmovanje za status, tudi marsikaj, čemur sami pravijo načelnost.

Gon 04

Pripadnost in pleme

Razvili smo se za življenje v skupini. Kdor je brez skupine, je ogrožen: ljudje brez tesnih, trdnih odnosov imajo slabše zdravje in počutje. Družbena zavrnitev boli, saj se ob njej odzovejo deloma ista možganska področja kot ob telesni bolečini. Pregled 148 raziskav je pokazal, da so imeli ljudje z močnejšimi družbenimi vezmi v obdobju spremljanja za 50 odstotkov večjo verjetnost preživetja. Ta gon ustvarja plemena okoli vsega, kar zanesljivo zaznamuje identiteto: jezika, oblačil, religije, športnega kluba, politične stranke, poklica.

Gon 05

Smisel in pomembnost

Ko so temeljne potrebe zadovoljene, smisel postane ozko grlo. Trije viri so dobro dokumentirani: pripadnost nečemu večjemu od sebe, prispevanje z naporom in skladna pripoved o lastnem življenju. Kadar so prisotni vsi trije, je smisla veliko. Kadar kateri koli manjka, smisel omahuje.

Gon 06

Razumevanje in mojstrstvo

Radovednost v svoji površinski obliki, mojstrstvo v svoji daljši. Povezano z dopaminom, ki se najmočneje odzove, kadar je izid boljši od pričakovanega. Radovednost je krhka, ugasnejo jo dolgočasje, poniževanje ali prezgodnji odgovori. Mojstrstvo terja namensko vajo na robu trenutne zmožnosti. Temelj vsakega človeškega dosežka v znanosti, umetnosti, obrti in športu.

Gon 07

Avtonomija in nadzor

Želja biti vzrok lastnih dejanj, ne predmet tujih. Stabilna prek kultur. Analiza 259 raziskav kaže, da zasnova dela pojasni približno tretjino zadovoljstva z delom, k zasnovi pa sodi tudi to, koliko lahko ljudje sami odločajo, kako, kdaj in kje delajo. Korelacija med plačo in zadovoljstvom je komaj 0,15. Gon po avtonomiji je v napetosti s pripadnostjo; zdravo odraslo življenje je pogajanje med obema.

Gon 08

Udobje in ugodje

Najpreprostejši gon in tisti, ki ga naše sodobno okolje najuspešneje ugrabi. Dopaminski sistem nagrajevanja se je razvil, da je možganom sporočal »temu je vredno slediti«. V svetu obilja so izdelki zasnovani tako, da ta sistem izkoriščajo: industrijsko predelana hrana, algoritemski viri vsebin, igre na srečo, pornografija. Ali je pretirana raba že zasvojenost, je sporno. Gon ni patološki. Spremenilo se je okolje.

Iz 4. poglavja. Goni
Univerzalije

Lastnosti, prisotne v vsaki preučeni človeški družbi.

Leta 1991 je antropolog Donald Brown objavil seznam človeških univerzalij. Na stotine postavk, ki jih delijo vse skrbno opazovane kulture. Če se neka lastnost pojavi pri predagrarnih lovcih in nabiralcih, v starodavnih sumerskih mestih, na srednjeveškem Kitajskem in v sodobnem Manhattnu, je del človeške narave, ne kulturno naključje.

Kaj to pomeni

Religija, hierarhija, spolne vloge, lastnina, umetnost, pripovedništvo in konflikt so univerzalni po obliki, raznoliki v podrobnostih. Nikdar ni bila dokumentirana družba brez nadnaravnih verovanj. Prav tako ne družba brez statusnih razlik. Strogih trditev 20. stoletja, da je spol povsem konstruiran ali da je lastnina umeten pojav, medkulturni zapis ne podpira. Tudi nasprotne, biološko deterministične trditve ne vzdržijo. Resnični sta obe, biologija in kultura, in obe opravljata pomembno delo.

Individualna variabilnost

Znotraj univerzalij velika peterica.

Pet razsežnosti osebnosti, ki se pokažejo v večini preučenih velikih družb (ne pa pri Tsimane, bolivijskem ljudstvu nabiralcev in poljedelcev). Dednost vsake je približno 40 do 60 odstotkov. V odrasli dobi so razmeroma stabilne, s starostjo še bolj, a nikoli povsem okamnele. Lastnost vam določa izhodišče, ne pa tega, kdo ste. Bolje se obnese, če delate z njo, kot če se borite proti njej.

Pogoste napake

Pet napak, ob katere se boste spotaknili.

Ko začnete raziskovati človeško naravo, boste naleteli na te vzorce zmot. Vsak od njih je napol pravilen in nevaren, kadar ga vzamete za celovito razlago. Če jih poimenujete vnaprej, preprečite večino škode.

Napaka 01

Nepopisan list

Trditev, da se ljudje rodimo brez nagnjenj in da vse vedenje izvira iz kulture. V veliki meri napačna. Rodimo se z bogatimi razvitimi nagnjenji: prirojeno pozornostjo do obrazov, vrstnim redom usvajanja jezika, hitrim zaznavanjem kač, moralnimi intuicijami. Kultura gradi na tem ogrodju, včasih dramatično. Ne piše na prazno stran.

Napaka 02

Biološki determinizem

Nasprotna zmota. Trditev, da biologija v celoti določa vedenje in da je kultura zgolj okras. Prav tako napačna. Biologija postavi razpone in privzete vrednosti. Znotraj teh razponov ima kultura ogromno moč. Resnični sta obe in za razlago česar koli potrebujete obe.

Napaka 03

Plemeniti divjak

Zamisel, da so predcivilizacijski ljudje živeli v miru in da nas je civilizacija pokvarila. Lovsko-nabiralniško življenje je imelo resnične prednosti: enakopravnost, zdravo prehrano, močne vezi. Bilo pa je tudi resnično nasilje, otroci so pogosto umirali, medicine ni bilo. Strokovnjaki si niso enotni, koliko tega nasilja je bilo organiziranega vojskovanja in koliko ubojev med posamezniki. Ni bilo raj.

Napaka 04

Sodobni ljudje smo drugačni

Zrcalna slika. Trditev, da smo kategorično drugačni od ljudi izpred 2.000 ali 20.000 let zaradi iPhonov ali znanosti. Ne drži. Naravni izbor je deloval še naprej, a naši osnovni goni, čustva in družbene potrebe so prepoznavno enaki kot pri kmetu iz bronaste dobe. Preberite Homerja in prepoznali boste sebe, čeprav se načini razmišljanja in moralne prioritete med populacijami razlikujejo.

Napaka 05

Vse pojasnjuje eno samo gonilo

Skušnjava, da vedenje zvedete na eno samo gospodujočo spremenljivko. Spolnost, denar, status, strah pred smrtjo, moč, razredni boj, racionalna izbira. Vsak velik teoretik, ki je to poskusil, ima deloma prav in v veliki meri narobe. Človeško vedenje je naddoločeno. Vselej je dejavnih več gonov. Teorija, ki vse zvede na eno samo gonilo, bo zgrešila večino slike.

Dodatek

Romantiziranje prihodnosti

Napredek je resničen, ni pa zajamčen. Tehnologija ne izboljša samodejno človeške blaginje. Nedavne tehnologije (prirejena hrana, družbena omrežja, aplikacije za zmenke) so poleg svojih koristi prinesle znatne nove škode. Predpostavite, da bo z naslednjo tehnologijo enako, tudi če vam je všeč.

Stabilnost in sprememba

Kaj zgodovina spreminja in česa ne.

Večina stabilnih značilnosti vašega trenutnega življenja je odvisna od nadaljnjega delovanja infrastrukture sodelovanja. Večina stabilnih značilnosti vašega notranjega življenja je odvisna od nadaljnjega delovanja vaše biologije. Računajte, da bo drugo zanesljivejše od prvega.

Se je dramatično spremenilo

Razmere

Materialne razmere (hrana, zavetje, medicina, prevoz). Hitrost komuniciranja. Povprečna življenjska doba in umrljivost dojenčkov. Suženjstvo, formalna hierarhija, pričakovanja glede spola. Politične ustanove (demokracija, državljanstvo, pravice). Verska krajina. Dolgoročne stopnje nasilja (Pinker dokumentira njihov upad, nekateri statistiki pa to izpodbijajo).

Vse to se je skozi stoletja izjemno premaknilo. Večina tega, kar se pri vaši trenutni družbi zdi trajno, je novo in odvisno od okoliščin.

Goni: status, spolnost, pripadnost, smisel, udobje. Osnovna čustvena paleta. Kognitivna arhitektura (hitra, samodejna presoja prehiteva počasno, preudarno razmišljanje, velik del tega pa poteka nezavedno). Potreba po plemenu in obredu. Vzorci sodelovanja in odpadanja. Hrana

Povezanost

Informacijska dieta

Ostro omejite vsebine iz algoritemskih tokov. Nadomestite jih z izbranimi viri: knjigami, določenimi ustvarjalci, daljšimi oblikami. Telefon zunaj spalnice. Znova zgradite zmožnost za dolgčas. Ta je podlaga ustvarjalnosti.

Status

Premišljeno izberite svoje hierarhije. Kurirajte svoj vir vsebin tako, da odstranite neproduktivne primerjave. Naredite popis statusnih iger, ki jih igrate. Odločite se, v katere vlagati in iz katerih izstopiti.

08

Smisel

Praksa ali projekt, ki se vam zdi sam po sebi vreden truda, zavarovan v koledarju. Večina odraslih smisel podceni v prid nujnih zahtev. Cena je odložena in huda.

Pravi počitek, ne uživanje medijev. Sprehodi. Čas v naravi. Spanje. Savna. Dihalne vaje. Karkoli vam sklene cikel stresa. Sodobno življenje ga vedno znova odpira. Skleniti ga morate premišljeno.

Parjenje

Svoj odnos ali iskanje odnosa obravnavajte kot resen življenjski projekt, ne kot hobi. Izogibajte se nadomestkom, ki gon izčrpavajo, ne da bi ga potešili. Infrastruktura za stabilne partnerske vezi je oslabela. Potrebno je zavestno grajenje.

Te navade postavijo temelj, ozdravitve pa ne prinesejo. Resna duševna bolezen ima pogosto biološko ozadje, ki zahteva zdravljenje. Česar model ne razreši

Kje je ta model negotov.

Dober model poimenuje svoje meje. Nekaj poštenih negotovosti, ki jih velja nositi ob boku vsemu, kar preostanek dokumenta trdi z gotovostjo.

Mehanizem kognitivne revolucije

Je bil genetski, kulturni ali oboje? Dokazi nekaj nakazujejo, a niso dokončni. FOXP2, nekoč glavni osumljenec, ne pride več v poštev, mnogi arheologi pa namesto enkratnega preskoka vidijo postopno kopičenje. Mogoči odgovori so genetska sprememba, kulturni prag ali kombinacija obojega.

Zavest sama

Nobeden od modelov v izvorniku se ne dotakne trdega problema. Zakaj subjektivno izkustvo sploh obstaja, ostaja odprto, in večina tega, kar povemo o psihologiji, o tem molči.

Geni proti okolju, v vsakem posameznem primeru

Ocene dednosti na ravni populacije se ne prevedejo čisto na posamezne primere. Vedeti, da je neka lastnost v populaciji 50 odstotno dedna, vam ne pove tako rekoč ničesar o tem, kaj je mogoče za določenega človeka.

Ljudje, globoko vpeti v umetna okolja

Ljudje, ki so odrasli povsem na spletu, imajo morda drugače umerjene gone. Še ne vemo, kako je to videti v populacijskem obsegu. Prve znake je videti v podatkih o počutju mladostnikov, raziskovalci pa se ne strinjajo, koliko k temu prispevajo zasloni.

Učinek umetne inteligence na infrastrukturo sodelovanja

Denar, pogodbe, znamke, identiteta. Vse to lahko umetna inteligenca v naslednjih 10 do 20 letih preobrazi. Goni se ne bodo spremenili. Ustanove, ki jih usmerjajo, se bodo.

Ali ima cikel vzpona in zatona zdaj enako obliko

Nekateri zgodovinarji v zgodovini vidijo cikle. Sedanji trenutek se od vseh prejšnjih v nekaterih točkah razlikuje: globalna povezanost, tehnološko pospeševanje. Cikel se lahko stisne, raztegne ali spremeni obliko.

Model je dovolj trden, da nanj stavite. Ni pa dovolj trden, da bi terjal gotovost. Temu ustrezno ga uporabljajte.

Ste biološki organizem z omejenim časom in naborom gonov, ki lahko, pravilno nahranjeni, porodijo razcvet.

Isti goni, pravilno razumljeni, lahko porodijo tudi življenje, ki je na videz natrpano, a znotraj prazno. Če dočakate 80 let, imate na voljo približno 4.000 tednov (Oliver Burkeman), precej pa ste jih že porabili. Preostali so končni in nepovrnljivi.

Prispevek, ki ga vedno znova odlagate, je tisti, ki ga boste obžalovali.
