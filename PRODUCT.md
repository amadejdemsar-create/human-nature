# PRODUCT

## Register

brand (long-form content: the page is the product)

## Purpose

A single-page bilingual (EN / SL) visual essay that explains human nature from first principles: biology, history, drives, universals, the modern mismatch, and a working model. Source of truth: `~/Domain/SecondBrain/Knowledge/topics/humanity/human-nature/`.

## Readers

Amadej and the people he shares it with (colleagues at Nevron and NativeAI, friends). They read it on a laptop or a phone, in one sitting or in pieces, to build a mental model they can use in business and in their own life. Some readers check where claims come from, so every load-bearing claim is backed by a listed source.

## Personality

Calm, serious, confident. Dark, night-reading surface with one warm gold accent; serif display headings over a clean sans body.

## Anti-references

Hedged academic papers; listicle self-help pages; SaaS landing-page scaffolding (metric heroes, card grids, eyebrows on every section).

## Principles

- Clarity first: one idea per section, depth on click (collapsible detail), never walls of citations in the body.
- Sources are visible but quiet: a reader who wants them finds them per chapter; a reader who does not is not slowed down.
- Both languages stay in parity: every change lands in EN and SL.
- Accessibility: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, works at 390 px, respects reduced motion.
